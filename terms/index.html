Terms

Last updated November 13, 2026

These terms apply when you use aguirrestowing.com or book a tow with Aguirre's Towing & Recovery LLC. Booking or paying for a tow means you agree to them.

Prices

  • A tow is $170 plus $5 for each mile from the pickup to the drop-off. Miles are rounded up.
  • Storage at our yard is $20 a day, billed when you pick the vehicle up.
  • The price page covers pickups and drop-offs within 20 miles of our yard in Sherwood. For anything farther, call and we quote it on the phone.
  • The price you see is the full price for the job as you described it.
  • If the job turns out to be different, for example the vehicle is off the road or stuck, it is a different vehicle, or an address changes, we tell you the new price before we start work. You can accept it or cancel.

Payment

  • Tows booked on the price page are paid before the truck is sent, by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. Stripe processes the payment and emails the receipt.
  • Tows booked by phone or text can be paid by card or cash at the scene.

Cancelling and refunds

  • Cancel before the truck is on its way and you get a full refund.
  • Cancel after the truck is on its way and we keep a $50 dispatch fee and refund the rest.
  • Once the vehicle is hooked up, the tow is not refundable.
  • If we cannot do the job for a reason on our side, you get a full refund.

To cancel, call or text (971) 329-0201. Refunds go back to the card you paid with. Your bank may take several days to show it.

Your part

  • You own the vehicle or have the owner's permission to have it towed.
  • The addresses, vehicle and phone number you give us are correct.
  • The truck can reach the vehicle. If you will not be there, tell us where the vehicle and the keys are.
  • Take valuables and personal items out of the vehicle before the tow.

Arrival times

Any arrival time we give is our best estimate. Traffic, weather and other calls can change it. If we are running late, we tell you.

Storage

A stored vehicle is released when storage and any other unpaid charges are paid. Vehicles left unclaimed are handled as Oregon law allows.

Damage and problems

  • We are a licensed and insured Oregon business.
  • If you think something was damaged, tell us at drop-off or as soon as you can, so we can look into it.
  • If you have a problem with a charge, call us and we will go over it with you. Nothing in these terms limits your right to dispute a charge with your card company.

The website

We may change our prices and these terms. The price shown when you pay is the price for that job. If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.

Governing law

Oregon law applies to these terms.
