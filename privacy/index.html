Privacy

Last updated November 13, 2026

This page says what information Aguirre's Towing & Recovery LLC collects through aguirrestowing.com, calls and texts, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • When you call or text: your phone number and what you tell us about the job.
  • When you use the price page: the pickup and drop-off addresses, the vehicle, your name, phone number and email, and any note you add.
  • When you pay online: Stripe handles the payment. We see your name, the amount and the last four digits of the card. We never see or store the full card number.
  • When you visit the site: which pages are viewed, which buttons are tapped, your browser and device type, and a rough location based on your internet address. We do not record what you type.

How we use it

  • To give you a price and do the job.
  • To call or text you about that job.
  • To take payment and send a receipt.
  • To keep business and tax records.
  • To see which parts of the site people use.

We do not sell your information. We do not use it for advertising. We do not send marketing texts or emails.

Companies that handle it for us

Each one gets only what it needs for its part.

  • Stripe: payments and receipts.
  • Google Maps: address suggestions and driving distance on the price page.
  • Cloudflare: runs the price calculator.
  • Resend: delivers the paid job details to our dispatch inbox.
  • PostHog: counts page views and button taps.
  • GitHub and Google Fonts: host the site and its lettering.

We also share information when the law requires it, such as a police or court request.

Cookies

We set no cookies. The price page saves one small layout setting in your browser, and it holds no personal information. Stripe may set its own cookies on the price page to help prevent fraud.

How long we keep it

  • Job and payment records are kept as long as we need them for business, tax and legal records.
  • If you get a price online and do not pay, the details stay with an unpaid record at Stripe.
  • Site visit counts are kept for about a year.

Your choices

Call or text us to ask what we have about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. We may need to keep records the law requires us to keep.

Children

This site is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.
